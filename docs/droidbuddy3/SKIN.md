# Droid Buddy 3 skin

`skin.droidbuddy3` is a remote-friendly Kodi skin derived from Estuary. It keeps
Estuary's player, library, PVR, games, settings, accessibility and dialog views,
while replacing the home experience with the Droid Buddy 3 launcher.

## Home controls

The 15 home buttons are arranged as a five-by-three grid with explicit remote
navigation in every direction. The default focus is **Movies & TV** so opening
the skin never preselects the power action.

The home destinations use Kodi built-ins and do not bundle media sources,
services or third-party add-ons. Users retain control over every source and
add-on they install.
